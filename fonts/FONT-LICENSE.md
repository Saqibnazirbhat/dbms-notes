# et-book font

The `et-book` typeface in this folder is the open-source webfont version of the
typeface used in Edward Tufte's books, created by Dmitry Krasny, Bonnie Scranton,
and Edward Tufte, and converted to webfont form by Adam Schwartz.

It is distributed under the MIT License by its authors.
Source: https://github.com/edwardtufte/et-book
